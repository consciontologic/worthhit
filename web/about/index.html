worthhit

Evidence before estimates.

Worthhit helps buyers and sellers understand a second-hand asking price. Give it the item, condition, market, currency and dated comparable listings. It checks the evidence before showing a price range.

How a price check works

  1. Describe the item honestly, including condition, defects, age, accessories and location.
  2. Paste dated comparable listing excerpts with their sources, or explicitly enable paid web research through OpenRouter.
  3. Review the accepted comparables, the resulting range, cautions and questions. Missing or unsupported evidence can prevent an estimate.

A listed asking price is not proof of a completed sale. Local demand, transaction costs, condition and timing all affect what someone may actually pay. Worthhit separates these limitations rather than promising an exact universal price.

Fair negotiation, without pressure

The appraisal instructions account for attachment to possessions, anchoring, urgency and uncertainty. Guidance should help both sides explain a reasonable offer, inspect the item and reduce mistrust. It must not exploit vulnerability or invent scarcity, competing offers or evidence.

Inflation, currency changes and popularity need dated, relevant support. They are not automatic percentage adjustments. AI output can still be wrong: inspect the cited material and use your own judgment before paying.

Your data and connection

Worthhit runs in your browser without an application database or API proxy. Drafts, validated reports and preferences are stored on your device. Your OpenRouter API key is held in memory for the current session.

When you request an appraisal, your submitted content goes directly to OpenRouter and the selected provider. Paid web research may also involve a search provider. Their policies apply to those requests. Avoid confidential information, personal contact details and anything you do not have permission to share.

Static hosting receives ordinary web requests and may keep infrastructure logs. Worthhit does not add advertising trackers or analytics. Language selection uses browser preferences, with a manual override; it does not send your IP address to a geolocation service.

Changing from the old project URL to worthhit.com creates a new browser storage origin. Export any report you want to keep from the old site before switching; browser storage cannot automatically move between domains.

Common questions

Is Worthhit free?

The app has no subscription. You bring your own OpenRouter key. The model selector accepts eligible free routes, subject to provider availability and rate limits. Optional web research can cost money and requires your explicit selection.

What can I check?

Lawful second-hand items with enough market evidence. Worthhit rejects prohibited categories including weapons, illicit drugs, pornography, stolen goods and confidential documents. A result is a decision aid, not a certified appraisal or a guarantee of a sale.

Which languages are available?

English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português, العربية and 简体中文. The app follows supported browser language preferences, or you can choose a language in Settings. Original evidence quotations remain unchanged.

Does it work offline?

After the complete app shell has been cached successfully, you can reopen it to work on a local draft or read a saved report. New model discovery, provider checks and appraisals require an internet connection. Installation and storage availability depend on your browser.

Can I install it?

Use your browser's install or add-to-home-screen option when available. Worthhit adapts its workspace to compact and wider screens and supports system, light and dark appearance.
